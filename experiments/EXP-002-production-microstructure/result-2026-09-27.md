## Limitations

- Five-second sampling is not event-complete; observed persistence cannot establish continuous exchange-level duration.
- Production-public REST polling, not production WebSockets. Across-market snapshots and metadata/book reads are not simultaneous. Fresh means within the existing recorder stale threshold; a recently confirmed book can be reused between successful polls.
- Displayed liquidity is not guaranteed fill quantity; there is no queue-position information, order placement, or fill simulator.
- Fees and trade P&L are not modeled. The post-quote proxy is not a trade result.
- The unchanged bounded candidate universe is ranked within at most three pages, not the exchange-wide active universe. Fixed selected markets can close or become one-sided during the run; eligibility and concentration must be considered.
- All four qualifying contracts concern one underlying game/player, so their behavior can be correlated. A separate-event replication is needed before generalizing the verdict.
- Overlapping forward windows are dependent. Bucket averages can reflect market mix, event timing, and outliers; small buckets and one session cannot validate forecasts or establish out-of-sample stability.
- Observation-weighted aggregate summaries can hide concentration. Window-boundary episodes are censored and durations are conservative. Exact price classifications and integer imbalance boundaries are retained; extremely large displayed-quantity summary numbers are approximate.
