## Anomalies and conclusion

- The first clean-SHA run failed coverage at 89.56%; its original rows and
  measurements are preserved in the baseline failure record. The scheduler was
  corrected in commit `89d491b1d93b2015551a7b4e8215d2df0c07b5ab` and the full
  30-minute run was repeated.
- One candidate was excluded after a startup 404. A later metadata refresh also
  returned HTTP 404; recording continued without dropping the fixed universe.
- Five markets had more than 5% stale rows individually, although aggregate
  freshness exceeded the predefined 95% threshold.
- Seventeen markets had no non-null quote data during this run. Their confirmed
  empty books remain valid recorder observations under the stated semantics.
- No malformed messages, dropped snapshots, duplicate writes, source mixing,
  unexpected disconnected rows, or orders were observed.

**EXP-001 verdict: PASS WITH LIMITATIONS.** The corrected recorder met the
predefined aggregate coverage and freshness thresholds, preserved source and
counter integrity, recovered quickly from a controlled disconnect, and shut
down cleanly. It is reliable enough to proceed to microstructure/profitability
research using appropriately filtered data. Per-market staleness, empty books,
the metadata 404 diagnostic gap, and the lack of a live database-outage test
remain limitations. This conclusion is not evidence of profitability and demo
liquidity is not representative of production liquidity.
