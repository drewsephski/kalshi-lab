## Limits and next experiment

REST polling adds latency and can miss publication delays beyond the one-second overlap. Production WebSocket auth was not used. Books remain discrete five-second REST observations on a local receipt clock while trades carry exchange timestamps. Hidden or removed liquidity, cancellations versus executions, replenishment, and absent account-specific queue position prevent exchange-grade fill proof. Block trades are excluded. Direction was explicit in this sample, but a future missing or contradictory provider side remains unknown. Current event/series fees and an undated general PDF do not prove historical market-specific maker fees. The two completed sessions were adjacent and concentrated in active series; activity may not generalize. The first failed collector and earlier book outage are preserved above, not hidden.

Recommended EXP-005: pre-register longer, separated credential-free collection windows using the same deterministic universe selection, seek at least 50 candidate orders with relevant directed flow across independent events, and archive dated official event/series fee notices or a verified historical fee source. Re-run the unchanged queue evidence analysis first. Do not advance to maker-simulator-v2 P&L conclusions until the frozen queue and fee gates are met or a new protocol explicitly records why they cannot be met.
