## Primary strategy and fill assumptions

One passive YES buy at the observed best bid, one contract, spread >=2c, both best sizes >=10, valid whole-cent quotes, fresh book/ticker receipts <=7.5s, open/active and connected. Primary latency **1000ms**, entry expiry **30s**, target **original ask minus 1c**, holding period **60s**. One attempt per wide-spread episode, no overlap, and no episode reset from missing data alone.

Activation is delayed to the first fresh book after scheduled activation, within 7.5s; its snapshot cannot also fill the order. Already-marketable post-only orders are rejected. Missing evidence earns no hypothetical fill. Entry and exit use separate evidence. Queue-ahead measurements include displayed size at equal/better prices, with unknown ladder coverage retained as null.

- **Optimistic:** subsequent opposite quote at/through the limit, executable size >=1, and increasing cumulative volume with post-activation receipts. A generous fill proxy, not a guaranteed P&L upper bound.
- **Base:** opposite quote strictly through the limit on two consecutive refreshed observations, increasing volume on the confirming observation; assign the limit price without improvement. Still a proxy, not observed queue consumption.
- **Pessimistic:** no fills from these snapshots, because complete directed queue-depletion/trade evidence is unavailable. Displayed quantity decreases may be cancellations.

If passive exit does not fill before 60s, cancel and liquidate at the first fresh executable bid after deadline plus latency (within 7.5s), minus a 1c haircut. No available quote leaves an unresolved position that blocks subsequent entries and prevents a passing verdict.

## Fees

The official [Kalshi fee schedule](https://kalshi.com/docs/kalshi-fee-schedule.pdf), effective July 7, 2026 and checked September 28, specifies taker and maker quadratic fees and centicent rounding. Exact bigint arithmetic implements those formulas; whole-cent rounding is a separate conservative sensitivity because the PDF example tables retain whole-cent amounts. No discounts or refunds are assumed.

The six evaluation series returned `quadratic` and multiplier 1 in unsigned current API reads. However, the recorder did not archive historical event overrides, and current event metadata returned year-0001 update timestamps. The committed [fee evidence](fee-evidence-2026-09-28.json) therefore does not approve a historical fee window: the selection manifest contains no approved rules. Hypothetical completed trades stay in gross analysis with null fees/net rather than an invented free-maker/default-taker assumption. The tested fee engine can apply explicitly evidenced event/window rules in future runs.

Zero fees/net on a scenario with no hypothetical executions describe an empty realized ledger, not a profitable or validated strategy. When completed trades have unknown fees, aggregate fees/net are unavailable. No result here supports positive after-fee expectancy.
